The package setup (layer stack, materials, TIM, spreader and sink conventions) follows the HotSpot-HMS model files of
Hong et al., HPCA 2024. The floorplans and layer file in `thermal/geometry/` are generated by
`thermal/geometry/build_5stack.py`; no HotSpot-HMS file is copied.

## Data

- `thermal/data/measured/`, `dram/data/`: our own measurements on H100, H200 and B200 (nvidia-smi readings). Host
  names, GPU serials and UUIDs are removed.
- HBM-Power measurement files (`data/all_idd_measurements.csv`, `data/HBM3E_measurements.csv`) are fetched by
  `dram/setup/hbm_power.sh` and are not stored here.
- JESD238B.01 is cited by section and table number only; none of its text is reproduced.
